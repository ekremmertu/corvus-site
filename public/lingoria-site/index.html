How much does it cost?

The first 7 days are free. After that it's a yearly subscription; the current price is shown on the App Store. Family Sharing is supported.

How do I cancel?

From your Apple ID settings, any time. If you cancel during the trial, you're not charged.

I'm a complete beginner. Is it for me?

Yes. The road map starts at A1, and the AI chat gives hints in Turkish so even beginners can answer.

Is the app in Turkish?

Yes. Lingoria is built for Turkish speakers: explanations and hints are in Turkish, the practice is in English.

What happens to my data?

Data is encrypted in transit and stored on Supabase. There are no ads and no third-party trackers, and you can delete your account from inside the app.
