Four game modes

Mode 1 · Classic

Endless board.
One more round.

Place pieces, clear lines, beat your record. Out of moves? Watch a short ad to keep your score and carry on — your call, and at most twice a game.

Mode 2 · Timed

Two minutes.
Every line buys time.

The clock starts at 2:00. Each line you clear adds bonus seconds, so a good combo buys you more time.

Mode 3 · Cleanup

A full board.
Leave it empty.

The board starts full. Clear every block to finish the level — and each level gets a little harder.

Mode 4 · Daily Challenge

One puzzle a day.
Same pieces for everyone.

Every day brings one seeded puzzle, and every player gets the same pieces. Tap Daily on the main menu, play every day and keep your streak alive.

Questions,
answered.

Is it free?

Yes. BlokBom is free to download and play. Ads keep it free.

Do I need the internet?

No. The game plays fully offline, so it works in the metro, on a plane or anywhere without signal.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up. Open the app and play.

What happens when I run out of moves?

In Classic mode you can watch a short ad to keep your score and continue. It's your choice, and you can do it up to twice per game.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes. BlokBom runs on iPhone and iPad, in English, Turkish, French, German, Spanish and Portuguese.
